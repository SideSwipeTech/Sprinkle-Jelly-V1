# Analytics

**Status:** Reviewed product definition

## Purpose and ownership

Analytics is the shared measurement capability inside Home / Skills / Progress. It receives permitted activity facts, measures active time, maintains summaries and interprets qualifying evidence. Home and Skills are presentations of those capabilities, not the whole of Analytics.

A producing domain owns what happened. Analytics owns its defined interpretation. Profile, Home, WizBit, domain summaries and Administration read the appropriate supplied facts without computing competing versions or inspecting private source.

## Inputs and outputs

| Producer supplies | Analytics may derive or present |
|---|---|
| Confirmed activity identity, time, area and permitted classification | Activity buckets and lifetime/annual summaries |
| Eligible first accepted Practice outcome | Practice-weighted skill contribution |
| Qualifying timed Debug acceptance | Debug evidence, not evidence from an ordinary practice fix |
| Eligible finalized Assessment question outcome | Assessment-weighted contribution using the supplied value |
| Retraction or recorded correction | Recalculation of only the affected judgments |
| Unfinished position and owner-confirmed resumability | Global Continue |
| Authored reachable next-action mappings | Explainable next steps |

No input includes learner code, Notes, raw answers, project files, terminal output or generated prompts. Repeating the same fact cannot increase time, solves or evidence. Independently authored Challenge and Track problems stay distinct; repeated work on the same owned item does not multiply its contribution.

## Active time and retained summaries

Use Activity's single time definition: genuine engagement, visibility, idle behavior, verified playback, union of overlapping intervals and independent measurement ceilings. Waiting for the platform and reading record-only pages do not become learning time. Proctored tests keep their Assessment duration rather than generating active-learning time.

Attribute skill-bearing time to one primary skill and its group. Secondary classifications do not duplicate the minute. General Code Lab, Solutions and Notes activity has no invented primary skill. A missing report never becomes a guessed duration.

Retain the lasting daily, annual and lifetime facts before shorter-lived detail expires. Do not recalculate lifetime work from only the detail still available. Current unfinalized activity, measured summaries and estimates must remain distinguishable. Retention and repair preserve the explicit Activity rules rather than silently revising sealed history.

## Skill evidence

[Skills](../domains/Progress/Skills.md#values-weights-and-minimum-evidence) owns values, weights, minimum evidence, freshness, bands, rounding, deduplication and trend calculations. This document does not create a second editable scoring table.

Evidence carries its owned item, source, captured classification/difficulty, occurrence time, eligible value and outcome reason. Classifications are not silently replaced with today's content labels. A deliberate classification merge preserves explainability rather than merging independently owned problems.

Assessment supplies the approved question value from its original grading: negative question marks can become zero evidence without changing actual test marks. Valid eligible unanswered/skipped/not-reached questions supply zero with their reason. Pending, faulted, invalidated, unclassified, staff/test and other excluded work supplies no invented contribution. Analytics does not re-grade answers or infer question results from a paper total.

Study, course quizzes, self-confirmed projects, XP, certificates, hints, Code Lab and Solutions browsing are not independent skill evidence. Evidence expiry removes current influence, not the earned solve or its readable historical fact.

## Shared readings and explanations

| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Current skill figure and band | Enough valid evidence supports the defined calculation |
| Not enough evidence | Show the actual shortfall, not a provisional ability of zero |
| Studied but unproven | Attributed study exists without qualifying assessment/practice |
| Nothing yet | No relevant recorded activity |
| Unavailable | The platform cannot establish the reading |

A trend uses the agreed dated-evidence comparison, not activity volume. An expired evidence window is not proof of declining ability. Why this result? must reconcile with the judgment and explain included/excluded evidence without revealing hidden material. If only the explanation fails, a trustworthy figure may remain; if its evidence does not support it, withhold the judgment.

A producer's measurement quality and freshness stay visible in understandable wording. The time a page was opened is not the time a figure was calculated. Another page cannot turn an estimate into measured precision or use a stale total to authorize access/spending.

## Facts owned elsewhere

Daily owns the streak and neutral-date rules. Economy owns XP, levels and achievements. Assessments owns Company readiness, recent form and Mock month-to-month change. Solutions owns accepted-entry counts, which can differ from distinct-problem counts. Certificates owns award validity.

Analytics may store the durable contributions needed for its summaries and display those owner-produced figures; it does not replace their formulas. Per-problem Solve/Fix rates retain the owning area's population. Personal accepted-submission statistics are different from a distinct-learner problem Solve rate. Show their denominators clearly.

## Continue, next actions and staff reporting

Global Continue chooses the most recently active unfinished work the owner confirms can resume. An archived enrolled subject or an already-started retired paper may still qualify. Historical completed results and withdrawn Practice problems do not. A failed eligibility read must not become a guessed destination.

Next actions use the approved authored mappings and Learn, Practise, Assess, Applied-work sequence. They are explainable links, not gates or generated plans. WizBit's proactive delivery has its own stricter nudge admission rules.

Staff reports receive bounded aggregate participation and content-review signals, not an individual learner surveillance feed. Exclude staff/test activity appropriately. A content flag is an inspection prompt, not a verdict that material is defective or a learner cheated. Permitted support reads remain the separate audited exception in Administration.

## Failure and checks

A failed summary, optional analytics write or reading does not reverse the producer's valid solve, award or completion. Preserve durable source facts so recoverable work is not silently lost. Missing telemetry is reported honestly rather than invented later. Explicit resets, retractions and erasure have their stated scopes and do not acquire permission to destroy other owners' records.

Check duplicate facts, overlapping tabs, date boundaries, independent problems, first versus repeated acceptance, negative/unanswered Assessment evidence, insufficient/stale data, retirement-aware Continue and matching figures across pages. Verify old recaps after detail expiry and private evidence explanations. No shared report should introduce a new reward, skill claim or learner ranking.
