## Purpose and ownership

One recorded-test capability serves Mock and Company Tests. It owns entry, captured conditions, answering, timing, proctoring, submission, grading coordination and result state. Reusing it does not require sharing questions between independently authored papers or adding a central question bank.

The paper supplies its type, content, sections, marks, availability, movement, review and proctoring settings. Access supplies the verified learner and session restrictions. Execution/Evaluation supply allowed runs and grading outcomes. The test capability preserves those facts and publishes only the outcomes its consumers may use.

## What is common and what differs

| Shared test behavior | Type-specific behavior remains with the domain |
|---|---|
| Start/resume and pre-test systems check | Mock catalogue versus company/role catalogue |
| Original questions and settings retained for the attempt | Mock navigation flags versus Company sectional policy |
| Per-answer saving and one deadline | Mock pass threshold and recognition versus Company readiness |
| Browser-event proctoring and its response | Company Actual/Pattern label and company-owned paper details |
| One submission/finalized result and recovery | Each type's review, history, messages and reported figures |

There is one implementation of the common lifecycle, not independently drifting Mock and Company clocks. Course quizzes may reuse question controls/evaluation but retain their unproctored temporary-take rules. Debug timed windows remain Debug's practice capability. Neither becomes a recorded Assessment simply because a timer appears.

## Starting and working

**Journey:** Read paper details → run the advisory readiness check → acknowledge the stated conditions → Start → answer and save → submit → receive final or pending result → permitted review/practice.

Opening a card or readiness check starts nothing. Start checks publication/availability, required content and services, session life and maintenance conditions. A failed start consumes no attempt. Repeated Start returns the same unfinished test. Readiness warnings are explicit; an unreadable check is not a pass.

Capture original questions, options, keys/cases, marks, timing, language/runtime conditions and applicable restrictions when the test starts. Later editing, archival or catalogue deletion does not change that attempt or destroy the material it needs. Reveal flags are read at review time under the owning policy, not used to recalculate marks.

Answer controls and keyboard navigation obey the paper's captured movement rules. A palette cannot reopen a locked section. Suggested section durations are pacing information, not a second section deadline. Visible-case coding Run is not final paper submission and obeys any section allowance.

## Time, saving and session protection

The authoritative deadline is fixed at Start. Refresh, disconnection, a hidden timer or leaving the page does not pause it. Submission grace changes when the platform finishes submission, never how long new answers may be written.

Every answer reports Saving, Saved, Saved on this device or a genuine failed-save retry. Only acknowledged work is promised across a lost device. Returning restores the same test and confirmed position/answers; unacknowledged typing can return only from the device holding it and while answering remains permitted.

A live recorded test blocks another sign-in without prompting or interrupting its device. Ordinary access lapse and an explicit security action have different consequences. Apply Access and the test's safe-ending rule together, rather than letting another page invent a new ending.

## Proctoring

Both types use Off, Standard or Strict. Off records no listed events; Standard records and warns; Strict can submit at the configured counted-event limit. The domain document owns the exact kinds, limits, optional observation settings and duplicate-event window.

Events describe browser observations, not proven intent. There is no camera, microphone, screen recording, clipboard-body or keystroke capture. Accessibility actions and platform/network faults are not misconduct. Help and Notes restrictions continue during a recorded test even with event recording Off; another tab cannot bypass them.

## Ending, grading and evidence

A manual submission or the permitted automatic ending produces one result. The reason is preserved: learner submitted, time ended, counted-event limit reached or a safe security ending. A disconnected device alone is not an ending. Unsafe security termination uses the existing invalidation rule.

Preserve the obligation to grade acknowledged answers. During grading, show no invented score, pass result, reward or readiness. Missing original cases are a platform/content fault: recover them or invalidate if provably irreparable. Never grade against a newly edited paper or turn missing cases into learner inactivity. Staff have no arbitrary score editor or general Retry grade control.

Results owns the full marking rules and worked examples. Assessment sends each eligible question's approved 0–100 evidence value to Analytics while keeping original marks unchanged. Valid eligible unanswered/not-reached questions contribute zero with their reason; pending or platform-failed work contributes no invented zero. Invalidation and provable repair trigger their owner-defined retractions/corrections.

Mock reward occasions go to Economy. Company readiness and recent form remain Assessment-owned calculations, not new Analytics formulas. Notifications receives the actual eligible event and safe wording, not private staff notes.

## History and further practice

Each paper has one current recorded result per learner under the agreed policy. Unlimited unrecorded practice opens after finalization and changes no history, skills or rewards. Invalidation or a permitted fresh-test grant supplies the defined exception; ordinary edits do not invent another attempt.

Old results, original retained material and current measured-result selection follow Results. Detail expiry leaves an honest summary, not a dead review link. A deleted catalogue item cannot be newly started merely because its old result is retained. Continue may still resume an existing valid unfinished test when the domain permits it.

## Consumer checks

Run the same start, save, deadline, duplicate-submission, proctoring and recovery cases for both types. Edit/remove the paper during an attempt and preserve its original grading. Verify permitted differences in navigation, rewards and Company figures, expired detail, new-test grants, unanswered evidence and temporary failures. A shared evaluator does not imply a shared quiz/test policy.
