## Shared responsibility

Economy provides one reward, XP, level, achievement and Credit capability. Activity domains confirm the qualifying event; Economy applies its approved policy. Other pages display its supplied values rather than calculating a second balance or granting points themselves.

XP recognizes activity, achievements recognize defined milestones and Credits fund optional generated assistance. None is a skill score, purchase currency or substitute for membership. Shared use adds no new reward type.

## Reward input and outcome

The activity supplies the verified learner, originating area, independent item, qualifying event and captured difficulty or other approved award facts. It cannot supply an arbitrary payment amount or claim another area's completion. Economy resolves the applicable amount and returns its actual award/delivery state.

**Journey:** Record qualifying activity and its reward occasion → apply the occasion once → update its balance/recognition → display confirmed state → deliver the permitted notice separately.

A Challenge and separately authored Track problem can earn independently. Re-solving the same owned problem, another language, refresh or a progress reset does not re-arm its first-solve award. A Track completion badge is not another payment for its problem solves. Daily bonus/catch-up rules and Debug's first-fix rule remain with their sources.

The award table, level curve, achievement conditions and allowed configuration ranges remain in the Economy domain documents. A consumer reads those values; it does not copy launch numbers into its own policy. A rate change affects later qualifying occasions, not a past stamped award.

## Recognition and display

Return total XP, current level and next-level progress from the same owner. A missing balance cannot become zero and mismatched totals cannot be silently repaired by the header. Level-cap presentation comes from Economy while XP continues to accumulate.

Keep a solve, reward, achievement, animation and notification distinct. A notification failure does not reverse XP. Quiet or unavailable WizBit does not prevent earning. A combined Daily streak/achievement event follows the approved single-notice treatment rather than announcing the same milestone twice.

Company Tests, Code Lab runs, individual lesson actions, video-subject completion and self-confirmed project work do not acquire XP because a shared award function exists. Each source uses only its declared reward occasions. No page offers Claim, discretionary gifts or a reward based on dwell time.

## Credit use

The three approved generated actions are the only spenders. The invoking page requests the current price and available balance, shows both, obtains explicit confirmation, and uses that confirmed request through the shared reservation/settlement flow.

A repeated request does not reserve or charge twice. An unseen price change requires renewed confirmation. Insufficient balance explains the action cost, available amount and next refresh while ordinary learning remains usable. A provider failure follows the owning release/refund rule rather than becoming a second purchase attempt.

The membership-month cycle, base allowance, automatic continuation allowance, expiry, holds and corrections are defined in Credits. A calendar-month provider limit is a separate Help control. Sufficient Credits do not override a restricted context, simultaneous-request limit or provider cap.

When generated help is disabled, Credit displays, history and priced controls follow the shared hidden-state rule together. XP remains visible. Credits are not sold, transferred, earned through practice or converted from XP.

## Corrections, privacy and failure

A pending award is not paid. Persistently failed awards use the original occasion and specific retry, not a replacement grant. Authorized corrections repair verified accounting faults with attribution and an incident; they do not create a score, solve or achievement. Invalidation reversals preserve their original history.

Account erasure removes the learner's economy data through its owner. Reports expose permitted aggregate delivery/cost information, not a ranking or a means to inspect private source. Consumers cannot alter balances, reservations or reward history directly.

## Checks

Exercise changed prices, insufficient Credits, one live generated request, release/retry, cycle boundaries and AI-off visibility. Every balance shown elsewhere must agree with Economy's supplied facts.
