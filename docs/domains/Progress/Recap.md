## Purpose and availability

The Annual Recap is a private, read-only account of one learner's recorded year. It describes their work patterns and achievements without comparing them with other learners or generating claims about proficiency.

A year qualifies after at least three accepted solutions and three active days. Its season runs from 15 December of that year through 31 January of the following year, on the product clock. There is no year-to-date recap outside this season.

During December, state the date through which the current year's figures are included. From 1 January, Home features the year just ended. A previously eligible year remains viewable later. Future years are refused; a year before the account existed gets its own explanation rather than being described as failed eligibility.

Home shows an eligible recap teaser, or names the eligibility requirement still short while in season. Outside the season, the teaser is absent. Dismissing it is remembered for that learner and year and does not dismiss next year's recap.

**Journey:** See the seasonal teaser → open the recap → read the year's story → return to learning. A later visit can reopen an eligible past year without making a public link.

## Story sections

One scroll contains the following sections in order. Omit any section with no meaningful figure rather than padding it with zeros. Omitting one does not reorder the others.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| Coder type | One rule-based description of the learner's recorded pattern |
| Solves by difficulty | Accepted solutions in the year by difficulty when solved |
| Languages | Accepted solutions by language and the most used language |
| Rhythm | Active days and busiest month by active days |
| Streaks | Daily's recorded longest streak and milestone tiers crossed in the year |
| Rewards | Actual achievement unlocks and XP award entries in the year |
| Lessons and accuracy | Lessons completed and accepted-submission outcomes divided by valid submissions |
| Milestones | The first solve, a qualifying biggest solving day and actual achievement unlocks |
| Year over year | Change from the previous year where there is a meaningful nonzero baseline |

Language and difficulty breakdowns count the relevant accepted records, not an inferred unique-problem total. Preserve independent originating areas. No submissions means no submission-accuracy percentage; it is not 0%. No previous-year solves means no percentage-growth comparison; it is not infinite growth.

Use the retained annual summary and the relevant owning domains' durable facts. Old recap years remain meaningful after detailed activity rows or accepted source code expire. An unavailable value is omitted or explained appropriately, never replaced with a made-up milestone.

## Coder types

Select the first matching rule below; do not assign several types. These are playful work-pattern labels, not psychological classifications or skill judgments.

| Order | Label | Qualifying pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Night Coder | At least 40% of accepted solutions at night, with at least five night solves |
| 2 | Weekend Warrior | At least 50% on Saturday or Sunday, with at least five weekend solves |
| 3 | Perfectionist | At least 90% accepted-submission accuracy across at least ten valid submissions |
| 4 | Steady Builder | At least 24 active days |
| 5 | Speed Runner | At least three accepted solutions per active day and at least twelve during the year |
| 6 | Grinder | None of the earlier rules matched and the required information is available |

Night means 22:00 through 04:59 on the product clock; weekend uses that same clock. Each label has a short authored explanation. Missing inputs do not automatically classify someone as Grinder. When the required information cannot be established, claim no type.

## Milestones, rewards and privacy

Milestones come only from the first solve of the year, the biggest solving day when it contains at least two solves, and achievement unlocks supplied by Economy. Do not invent milestones from routine browsing, Credits, a level change or a lesson just to fill the story.

Streaks and rewards keep their owners' definitions. XP corrections are not recast as new earned XP. Opening, completing or dismissing the recap grants nothing. A streak shown here is not a second annual streak calculation.

No public share address, downloadable evidence report, peer ranking, employment promise or AI-generated judgment is provided. A learner can view only their own recap. Account erasure removes its personal facts and dismissal state.

## States and completion checks

Distinguish out of season, in season but short of eligibility, eligible, a year before account creation and unavailable information. An unreadable source is not an inactive year. Hide the last section when it has no valid comparison just as with any other empty section.

Check both eligibility thresholds, December-to-January transition, a dismissed teaser, old eligible years, future years, each coder-type boundary and its precedence, missing inputs, zero previous-year solves and expired detail. Confirm that no recap wording implies a skill certification or compares learners.
