## Purpose

Skills explains what the learner's qualifying work on Labs material currently demonstrates. It provides an educational estimate, not certification of mastery, an employer endorsement or a hiring probability. XP, time spent and completion badges are not inputs to that estimate.

The learner can choose a period, inspect a skill's evidence, open Why this result? and follow an available suggested activity. There is no manual goal-setting, plan, target or personal checklist here.

## Page and periods

Show skill cards, improving and needs-practice lists, explainable guidance, at most one next action per skill, Assessment's company readiness and recent form, its Mock month-to-month comparison, descriptive practice patterns and How your stats work.

Periods are 7, 30, 90 and 365 days, with 30 days selected initially. There are no additional language, group or company filters. Company summaries use the company associated with the most recent relevant readiness/recent-form evidence in that period; this is not a claim that the company is the learner's chosen target.

Each result names its scope and freshness. Extending the selected period does not revive evidence already outside its source's freshness window. Different regions may be available independently.

## Qualifying evidence

| Source shown to the learner | What contributes |
|---|---|
| Challenges & Tracks | A first accepted solve of a classified, independently owned Challenge or Track problem |
| Daily Challenges | A first accepted solve of a classified Daily problem |
| Debug Detective | A qualifying timed-mode acceptance, not an ordinary practice-mode fix |
| Assessment — Mock | An eligible question outcome from a finalized recorded Mock |
| Assessment — Company | An eligible question outcome from a finalized recorded Company test |

Classifications and difficulty are recorded as they were when the activity occurred. A later content edit does not rewrite those historical facts. A skill merge may present related historical facts together, with the merge explained.

Each problem-owning area keeps its own items. A Challenge and separately authored Track problem can each contribute; matching wording never merges them. Re-solving the same owned item, another language on that item, a Run or a hint does not create another contribution. The source area and item remain visible in the explanation.

Exclude pending grades, invalidated outcomes, platform failures, previews, seeded activity and staff/test activity from learner evidence. Unclassified content does not produce a guessed skill association. Learning, course quizzes, Workspace self-completion, certificates, streaks, XP, Code Lab and Solutions browsing do not independently produce skill evidence.

## Values, weights and minimum evidence

| Rule | Value |
|---|---:|
| Practice contribution weight | 1 |
| Assessment question contribution weight | 3 |
| Minimum combined weight | 6 |
| Minimum distinct qualifying items | 2 |
| Practice freshness | 90 days |
| Assessment freshness | 180 days |
| Easy accepted practice value | 55 |
| Medium accepted practice value | 70 |
| Hard accepted practice value | 85 |
| Extreme accepted practice value | 100 |

The four practice values are configurable within 0–100 under their authorized settings; their meaning belongs here and they are not XP awards. Already captured values are not silently repriced. Assessment supplies the eligible 0–100 value under [Results — Skill evidence](../Assessments/Results.md#skill-evidence), derived from the question's original available marks and full-precision earned marks. Skills consumes that value; it does not re-grade an answer, inspect learner code or infer question correctness from an overall test score.

Full credit supplies 100, half credit 50, and a valid negative-mark answer supplies zero without changing its actual negative test marks. An otherwise eligible unanswered, skipped or not-reached question in a valid finalized test also supplies zero, with its reason retained. Missing grading material, pending work and invalidation supply no invented zero. The bounded evidence value does not alter Assessment accuracy, question marks, rewards or the test total.

Use the newest eligible contribution for each distinct owned item in the chosen skill and period. Multiply each value by its weight, add the weighted values and divide by total weight. Both the weight minimum and the distinct-item minimum must pass before displaying a judgment.

For example, two different eligible assessment questions have a combined weight of six. Six eligible practice problems also have weight six. The explanation names their different sources rather than implying identical evidence quality.

| Unrounded skill value | Label |
|---|---|
| Below 50 | Needs practice |
| At least 50 and below 75 | Developing |
| At least 75 | Strong |

Choose the label before rounding. Display the percentage to the nearest whole number, with an exact half rounded upward. A rounded display must not silently change the applied band. State whether all evidence came from practice.

## States that are not judgments

**Not enough evidence** shows the actual observed item count and total weight against their required minimums. It shows no convincing zero or provisional ability percentage. **Studied but unproven** describes attributed study time without qualifying practice or assessment and can offer an appropriate authored practice destination. **Nothing yet** describes no relevant record. **Unavailable** describes a failed read or calculation.

Evidence becoming older does not undo a solve, reward or certificate. Older evidence remains inspectable but stops contributing to the current estimate. Lack of recent evidence must not be labelled declining ability. A learner with no needs-practice skill should be offered an appropriate stretch direction where one exists, not a manufactured weakness.

## Trends and short lists

Take the selected period's dated evidence under the same eligibility, freshness and distinct-item rules as the skill figure. Order it oldest to newest, split it by count into an older and newer half, placing an odd middle item in the older half. Compare the halves' weighted means without rounding first.

A trend needs at least three dated eligible points spanning seven days. Movement of at least +5 points is Improving; movement of at most −5 is Declining; values strictly between are Stable. An invalid half or insufficient history yields no measured trend. Distinguish Not enough recent practice to read a trend from The trend could not be read just now.

The improving list is omitted when nothing qualifies. Each needs-practice row states its reason. Volume, time and coverage do not become strongest-skill or improvement judgments. Guidance states the applicable rule, including practice-only evidence or declining movement, and appears only with a reachable authored action.

## Evidence and Why this result?

A skill's evidence view shows source, item, date, relevant language, outcome, weight and freshness. Retracted evidence is excluded but identified as excluded; expired code is not needed to explain retained outcome facts.

Why this result? shows the scope and period, result, rule and threshold, included and excluded evidence with reasons, observed/required minimums, freshness and any classification or skill-merge effect. Use plain explanations and counts that reconcile with the displayed judgment. It is not reconstructed from the rounded percentage alone.

If the explanation temporarily fails while the result remains trustworthy, keep the result and offer retry inside the explanation. If the evidence cannot support the result, withhold the judgment and report a platform integrity problem, not a learner shortfall. A below-minimum card already explains its shortfall and does not need an empty receipt.

Do not expose hidden questions, cases, reference answers, learner source, proctoring-event detail, internal incidents or another learner's activity. These views are private and read-only; opening them creates no appeal, override, export or reward.

How your stats work is one authored panel available from the page header and result-quality labels. It explains evidence, labels, freshness and why repeated practice does not repeatedly raise a skill. It is the same general explanation for every learner, not generated analysis.

## Next actions

Offer zero or one authored destination per skill, based on the first missing stage in this order: Learn, Practise, Assess, Applied work. Missing study points to Learn; no practice evidence points to Practise; no assessment evidence points to Assess. Applied work is a possible destination, not a newly invented applied-evidence source or project grading system.

Use the evidence already held and the approved destination mappings. An archived, unavailable, unauthorized or prerequisite-blocked destination is not offered. Missing inputs or a valid destination produce no suggestion rather than a guess. The same facts and mappings produce the same result.

Following or dismissing guidance gates nothing and pays nothing by itself. A visible suggestion is not an unsolicited nudge; WizBit delivering it proactively follows the separate nudge restrictions.

## Descriptive practice patterns

These are personal submission statistics, not a problem's distinct-learner Solve rate. They use the chosen period and show their units and denominators without requiring the skill minimum.

| Figure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Outcomes by language | Accepted submission outcomes divided by valid submissions in that language during the period |
| Outcomes by difficulty | Accepted submission outcomes divided by valid submissions at the recorded difficulty during the period |
| Average active time to solve | Active time up to first acceptance across items first solved in the period, divided by those first solves |
| Average submissions to solve | Submissions up to and including first acceptance across those solved items, divided by their first solves |
| Days to first acceptance | Elapsed product-clock days from first submission to first acceptance for those items, divided by their first solves |

A first submission may predate the selected period. Use the actual submission outcomes, not the size of the first-accepted-code collection as a substitute numerator. A missing denominator displays a dash with an explanation, not 0% or zero minutes. Platform faults, free Runs and invalidated or excluded activity do not worsen these statistics. Hint usage stays neutral content-review information and is not a proficiency penalty.

## Assessment-owned summaries

Company readiness, Company recent form and Mock month-to-month assessed change remain owned by [Assessments](../Assessments/Assessments.md). Display their supplied values, bands, evidence minimums and explanations without another formula. No combined Mock-and-Company readiness score exists.

Readiness below its minimum says Not enough evidence, not Not ready. Missing Mock history is not zero change, and recent form is not renamed readiness. Why this result? for an Assessment-owned judgment uses that producer's explanation.

## Changes, staff actions and boundaries

Invalidation retracts the affected evidence and recalculates affected skills. A recorded score repair updates the affected evidence values without inventing a fresh attempt. Archiving content does not by itself retract a legitimate outcome. Staff may perform a previewed, reasoned and recorded skill-evidence reset; it changes only its declared scope and informs the learner. It must not erase solve history, accepted code, Economy records or certificates.

Authorized staff maintain authored next-action mappings and the bounded practice-value settings. Personal judgments are not a per-learner admin report. No public profile, comparison, rank, cohort, downloadable skill report, generated judgment or employment claim is introduced. Merely reading Skills earns no learning time.

## Completion checks

Compare every presented Assessment figure with its source. Verify that period changes neither revive stale evidence nor introduce a hidden company selector. Check full, partial, negative and unanswered question contributions against Results, preserving skipped/not-reached reasons and excluding unresolved grading faults rather than treating them as zero evidence.
