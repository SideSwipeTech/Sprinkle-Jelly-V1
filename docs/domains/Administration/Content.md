## Ownership and authoring

Administration hosts each area's management experience without owning another copy of its content or rules. Roles follow Administration: Admin prepares drafts and reviews submitted reports/requests; Super Admin publishes, applies live changes and performs protected content actions.

| Area | Managed content |
|---|---|
| Courses | Lessons and Video Courses, both Subject → Chapter → Lesson, including media, quizzes and assignments |
| Challenges | Independent multi-language problems |
| Tracks | Independent track-specific, single-language problems and their order |
| Daily Challenges | Independent dated problems, schedules and bonuses |
| Debug Detective | Independent broken programs, test cases, timed settings, bug types and debriefs |
| Assessments | Mock/Company papers, company details and roles, sections, questions and test settings |
| Workspace | Starter templates and their files, not learner projects |
| Worked solutions | Separately authored learner-safe explanations for an owning item and language |
| WizBit | Product knowledge, response wording and approved presentation |

One reusable coding-problem form serves the applicable areas. The area opening it determines the owner and destination. No central problem bank, live Challenge reference inside Tracks, synchronized edits or cross-domain completion is introduced. Related-item links are navigation only.

Create, read, update, permanently delete where safe, and Archive remain normal content capabilities. Published material can be edited on its existing item. There is no Trash, soft deletion or mandatory archive-and-duplicate correction loop. Each domain still protects active work, earned records and issued certificates.

## Common authoring tools

Show only the tools supported by the selected content: move, reorder, duplicate, restore an editing revision, preview, validate, compare revisions and import, alongside the domain's lifecycle actions. A tool supported by one domain does not automatically appear everywhere.

Incomplete drafts can be saved. Autosave protects unfinished editing and does not publish. Preserve typed input on failure or conflict; never silently choose between two authors' changes. A duplicate contains authored material only, without learner progress, submissions or rewards. A restoration returns earlier content to a draft/editor and must pass current checks before going live.

Preview uses the learner presentation without enrollment, solves, progress, awards or inbox activity. It does not become an unrestricted execution environment. Reference-run and template rehearsal capabilities remain the owning domain's validation, not permission to run everything from any preview.

Question imports offer their supported template, upload and paste controls. Validate the whole batch and identify row errors before writing. Import all valid proposed content or none; do not silently trim oversized input or accept only convenient rows. Course-content imports and Assessment question imports retain their distinct supported shapes. A shared importer does not change destination ownership.

## Publication and updates

**Journey:** Open the owning area → create or edit → save the draft → preview and validate → publish directly as Super Admin, or submit as Admin → approve and publish.

Approval is publication, not another required step before a second Publish button. Show the item, submitter, submitted changes, validation results, replaced material and impact. Missing validation or impact information blocks the decision where required. Rejection returns the draft with a reason. Inaction never approves it.

Approval applies to exactly the content reviewed. Further draft edits require renewed review; they must not slip into an already-reviewed submission. A failed publication leaves valid live content and unsaved author work intact. Live updates use the same validation as first publication.

Direct editing does not change historical learner facts. Assessment attempts keep their original questions, keys, timing and scoring. Daily's begun-date restrictions and Debug's affected-window recovery remain. A completed subject stays completed, and an issued certificate keeps its original award information. Template edits affect future creations rather than learner files.

Archive and Delete follow the owning domain's consequences. State affected children/references before a parent action. Keep required learner history and avoid dangling live links. If safe dependency checks or preserved records cannot be assured, refuse that deletion and offer Archive. Draft deletion and live-content removal follow the role policy, not a hidden maintenance-only menu.

## Change communication and curation

Published course changes notify eligible learners, including those who completed the subject. These notices are mandatory even when System notifications are off; individual autosaves do not send notices. A delivery failure does not reverse publication or erase its change record.

Assessment notices and fresh-attempt relief retain Assessment's separate rules. Other content follows its owning notification rule. Do not replace these with one blanket optional-announcement switch. When notice is optional, record the actual send/silence choice without inventing an audience. A fresh-attempt grant cannot quietly exist without its required notice.

Super Admin manages Home's one active curated suggestion: a permitted published learning/practice item, optional short explanation and recorded change reason. A withdrawn target clears the slot rather than producing a dead link. Authored Skills next-action mappings remain Progress-owned; they do not become a second Continue algorithm or a generated recommendation engine.

## Skills, topics and labels

Use one shared skill-to-topic picker. Content remains independently owned even when it shares a classification. Existing archived choices remain visible and labelled but are unavailable for new selection. A failed vocabulary read offers no invented free-text fallback and cannot pass publication checks.

Super Admin manages the shared hierarchy. Supported actions are Create, label-only Rename, Archive/retire and Merge. A label correction preserves the classification's identity, meaning, links and historical evidence; it is not a new skill. A change of meaning requires a separate classification or the explicit merge process, not a misleading rename.

A rename records old/new labels and reason. Renaming a topic does not move it to a different skill, merge evidence, rewrite old scores or change the meaning of an earned result. Where a historical record preserves its original label, keep that history rather than presenting a wording change as a new outcome.

Before Archive or Merge, show exact affected-content counts by domain with a way to inspect the named authored items. If the required counts cannot be read, confirmation waits. Repeated execution must not duplicate movement, and a failed merge leaves the vocabulary unchanged. Renaming and merging do not merge independently owned coding problems.

The lifetime vocabulary ceiling is 2,000 entries, counting active, archived and merged-source entries. Warn above 1,800; about 200 active names is findability guidance, not a publication gate. Archive and Merge do not free lifetime allowance. A rename creates no extra entry. Bulk creation is all-or-nothing. Permanent deletion and reactivation of classifications are not introduced.

The difficulty scale and language catalogue are read-only here. Debug's bug-type list stays in Debug management. Explain affected authored content when a runtime becomes unavailable while preserving language labels on recorded work.

## Sources and permissions to use content

Keep the effective author/owner, source, usage permission and permitted uses, dated citation where applicable, company-name/logo basis, third-party/generated contributions, reviewer/publication details and relevant dependencies. Defaults can be set for a container or validated import and overridden on individual items.

Before publication, show the effective source record and exceptions. Preserve the published record when a parent default later changes. This is content accountability, not a learner-facing version selector or a legal-advice workflow.

An Actual Company paper needs both a dated source and documented reproduction permission. Identify precisely what is missing. Pattern must not imply employer endorsement or possession of the original paper. Leaked, unauthorized or misrepresented material is not made acceptable through a label.

Worked solutions are authored separately from private grading references and learner submissions. Keep approach, explanation and complexity notes where appropriate, validate the example and retain the owning area's acceptance-based reveal rule. Missing learner-facing explanation is not permission to reveal a private reference answer.

## Content reports

Learners report existing content using Spam, Incorrect, Inappropriate or Other. A note is optional for the first three and required for Other. Only eligible detail pages offer Report; live-test and Workspace restrictions remain.

Staff review one queue, oldest first, with Open, Investigating, Resolved and Dismissed states. Domain filters narrow rows and status counts; a status filter narrows rows, not the cross-status breakdown. Totals and zero counts must be real.

Open and Investigating can move between each other or close. Dismiss requires a reason. Resolve records Corrected, No action, Escalated or Duplicate and may reference the correcting change. Resolved or Dismissed must reopen with a reason before another outcome; clear the former closure marker without losing history. Repeated moves do not create duplicate actions.

Link to the content's current editor and explain when it differs from the reported reference. Removed content does not remove the report. Do not imply that a current editor shows the exact historical screen. Correction uses the owning domain's normal validated update, not a report-specific publication bypass.

No assignment system, public moderation history or learner ranking is added. The report note is intentionally readable by staff; it does not grant access to the learner's other work. Report records keep their required history and sever reporter attribution on account erasure under the agreed retention rules.

## Requests, gaps and assistant content

Topic Requests retains its own permitted transitions, private team note and real fulfilment link. Submitted learner text cannot be rewritten by staff; its withdrawal, deletion and bulk-action restrictions remain. Admin may review requests; a protected override stays Super Admin-only.

Content Gaps keeps requested-content counts separate from recurring safe product-help search misses. Neither panel exposes requester identity or raw queries. One unavailable source does not blank the other, and search outages are not counted as demand. Each supported export follows the source's privacy and bounds.

Knowledge-base and response publication use WizBit's existing citation, coverage, review and restore checks. Super Admin controls live artwork, matching thresholds and bounded nudges; Admin can prepare authored changes. A staff role creates no direct channel to an individual learner through WizBit.

## Checks

Check reports after content changes/removal, mandatory course notices, original Assessment history, independent project copies and inaccessible private learner work.
