# People

**Status:** Reviewed  
[Administration](Administration.md)

## Find a person

Search the directory by name or email. Filter independently by the normalized Labs role, moderation standing and membership, so staff without learner membership and Users without staff access remain findable. The role filter has User, Admin and Super Admin only, all resolved from WordPress through Access.

The saved views are All users, Suspended or banned, and Accounts with a genuine main-site verification fault. Default to recently active records and use stable paging. No users, no matches and a failed lookup are different states. A match count is shown only when actually counted under the same filters.

Low Credits, low activity or weak performance do not create a behavioral needs-attention label. Membership lapse is not misconduct. There is no engagement-monitoring or discretionary-reward intervention system.

## Support information and role ownership

Admin and Super Admin may open the permitted, audited support picture: read-only identity/access facts, available headline and progress summaries, moderation standing/history and administrative actions involving the person. Identify an operator viewing their own account.

Show one Labs role and that WordPress controls it. Provide the appropriate main-site destination where permitted; there is no role grant, removal, invitation or permission editor in Labs. Removing a WordPress staff role changes subsequent authority through Access and does not remove historical accountability.

Name, email, avatar, membership and registration facts belong to the main site. A stale permitted mirror states its freshness; an unreadable one says unavailable rather than implying an inactive or banned account. Staff cannot change main-site identity or membership from this view.

Opening named-person detail, standing or history is a recorded read. If its required record cannot be written, do not disclose the protected view. Show operational information about learner work only through approved summaries, never by opening their source.

| Allowed run information | Not available to either staff role |
|---|---|
| Activity and language; run/submission reference | Submitted or saved accepted code |
| Time, duration, state and verdict | Typed input, clipboard content or terminal output |
| Visible-case count, not the hidden grading material | Private Notes and Workspace files |
| Resource use against limits and safe error class | Raw generated prompts, attachments or answers |
| Execution-service health and capture/grading completion | A raw download or impersonation route into that work |

Topic Request text, content-report notes and certificate-name proposals are explicit submitted-for-review exceptions. They are not permission to read other learner-written material. No staff action promotes learner code into a published editorial or template.

## Moderation

Only Super Admin changes a person's restrictions. Display Banned, Suspended until a stated date, or In good standing explicitly. Unknown history is not no history.

| Action | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Suspend | Reason, optional private note, and a stated duration/end; presets 1, 7, 30 or 90 days, custom 1–365 days |
| Lift early | Identify the active suspension, capture a reason and make the restorative effect clear |
| Ban | Reason and exact-person confirmation from the person's detail, not an unguarded list-row action |
| Unban | Typed confirmation and attribution; no reason required |

A restriction affects Labs access, not the main-site purchase. A private note never appears to the learner. The restricted page uses Access's factual wording and main-site contact route; it does not expose the moderator's identity, private reasons or reporter.

Account standing and required session consequences apply together. An affected live test follows Assessment's safe ending/invalidation behavior; staff do not choose a score. Automatic suspension expiry is shown as a platform transition linked to the originating restriction and creates no new learner notice. A role change is not itself a ban or erasure.

Self-suspension, self-banning and an administrative reset of the operator's own progress are blocked. General correction, role and restriction controls cannot bypass the shared recent-verification or attribution requirements.

## Proctoring review and Assessment actions

Admin and Super Admin can inspect the permitted Recorded Event review: actual count, whether flagged, which observations counted, ending reason and timeline. Keep it factual. A browser observation is not automatically upheld misconduct, and a clean test is not an unreadable timeline.

The review never exposes screen/audio/video recordings, clipboard bodies, learner source, hidden cases or inferred motive. It changes no score. Super Admin alone performs an allowed result invalidation, pre-start accommodation or fresh-attempt grant under the Assessment document; no role manually retries a pending grade or substitutes an arbitrary mark.

## Economy corrections

Super Admin may repair a verified missing, duplicated or mispriced XP/Credit record. Use Economy's focused correction, not a free-form grant. Require the incident, reason and explicit grant/deduct direction. Preview the current amount, signed change, resulting amount and resulting level when applicable.

Reject an excessive deduction instead of clamping it to zero, and reject an out-of-range amount instead of trimming it. If the current balance cannot be read, do not offer a guessed preview or confirmation. Append the explanatory correction and preserve the original record. Repeated confirmation applies it once.

This cannot create unsupported rewards, change Assessment scores, manufacture achievements or reopen paid first-solve awards. Failed-reward recovery uses Economy's existing action. Configuration and correction remain distinct.

## Progress reset

Only Super Admin performs a deliberate reset. Offer the nine independently selectable scopes below, not a generic delete-all-learning shortcut.

| Scope | Resettable material |
|---|---|
| Courses | Completion progress |
| Challenges and Tracks | Resettable progress for their independently owned items |
| Daily | Completion and streak state |
| Timed Debug | Timed progress |
| Mock Tests | Resettable Mock progress and associated evidence |
| Company Tests | Resettable Company progress, readiness and associated evidence |
| Workspace | Completion and checklist state, never files |
| Skills | Evidence and its derived judgments |
| Learning preferences and Continue | Relevant preferences and resume state, not identity/access |

Before confirmation, show selected scopes and exact affected counts. If a required count is unavailable, confirmation waits. Capture the reason, exact person and recent verification. A reset does not broaden itself after discovering related records.

Retain earned XP/level, the Economy ledger, permanent solve facts and paid reward identities, achievements, certificates, Notes and project files. Re-solving after reset earns no duplicate reward. An explicit reset is not an ordinary course update: content changes still cannot reset completed courses.

One recorded operation reports Pending, Running, Succeeded or Failed for each scope. Repeated confirmation returns it. Retry failed work safely without repeating successful steps; exhausted retries become a named operational incident. Do not call partial success complete. Send the one reset notice only once the requested result is consistent, naming scopes that changed and what survived; an empty reset sends no notice.

## Certificate actions

Super Admin handles certificate-name approval/rejection, explicit reissue, permitted revocation and failed-generation recovery. Admin may read only the safe support information required to route the issue; there is no separate Certificate Admin role or local permission grant.

Approve only the learner's exact current proposed name. Rejection leaves the existing value and provides its safe reason. Approval does not automatically rewrite issued documents. Reissue requires its own confirmation and affected count; a failed replacement leaves the earlier document usable.

Revocation uses Certificates' permitted grounds and history. It is not correction of a spelling error, ordinary course editing or a discretionary punishment. There is no arbitrary award, Delete certificate or staff-invented score. Generation retry uses the certificate-specific action rather than a generic job button.

## Confirm a payment reversal

Super Admin can record that the main site already processed a reversal. Require the exact account, reason and main-site reversal reference. Do not process the payment or store an amount, invoice or payment method here.

Apply Access and Certificates' approved consequences: the reversed entitlement is not reinstated by this operation, access ends as specified, and certificates funded by that invalidated entitlement follow the relevant revocation rule. A repeated confirmation is not a second reversal. This is not a billing dashboard, automatic reversal feed or new learner payment page.

## Account erasure

Self-service requests remain learner-controlled: seven days to cancel, then automatic processing. Neither Admin nor Super Admin approves, rejects or cancels one on the learner's behalf. The console supplies read-only progress and handles genuine operational failures through their permitted recovery rules.

Super Admin can initiate an administrative erasure with exact account confirmation, a mandatory reason and recent verification. It is scheduled without the learner's seven-day cancellation window. State that consequence before confirmation. One account has at most one unfinished request; another press returns it, not a parallel erasure.

List request source, state, relevant dates, per-step progress, safe failure information and oldest unfinished age. Held and Failed are different. Until the final required step succeeds, say Erasure in progress rather than Erased or Partially erased. Do not claim completion after only some data has gone.

Erasure removes personal records according to their owning rules and removes identifying certificate presentation so public checks no longer expose the person. Ordinary account closure or membership lapse does not have that certificate consequence. Preserve approved de-identified planning facts and protected administrative history; this is not a promise that every record in the platform disappears.

## Retention holds

Only Super Admin places or releases a genuine hold. Record the exact subject and covered data, written reason, named authorizer, start, review date and eventual end. Review at least every ninety days and require a renewed basis rather than silently keeping everything forever.

An applicable hold pauses erasure/deletion as required by the shared retention rule, not unrelated learning or account actions. Hold details remain confidential. Releasing the last applicable hold resumes affected work from the first unfinished step and reports the actual count. An already-released hold reports no change, and concurrent releases cannot lose the resume.

A hold never restores information already erased. It is not a general block-all-deletion preference. Account erasure does not depend on the separate authorization for age-based cleanup.
