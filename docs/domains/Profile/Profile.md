## Purpose and ownership

Profile brings together the learner's accumulated record. It is reached from the account menu and is private to that learner. It displays facts from their owning domains without defining another XP curve, streak, skill score or completion calculation.

Profile & Settings owns its account preferences and their presentation. Identity belongs to the main site; Certificates owns certificate names and awards; Notifications owns its delivery preferences; Progress owns shared activity and skill figures.

This is a product description, not the technical composition of the page.

## Profile regions

| Region | Content and owner |
|---|---|
| Identity header | Main-site display name and avatar, Daily's streak, and Economy's XP and level |
| Headline figures | The retained lifetime activity record from Progress |
| Difficulty breakdown | Solutions' accepted-entry counts by recorded difficulty |
| Compact skills card | The existing Skills figures, labels and appropriate explanation links |
| Group summaries | Learning completions, practice solves/fixes and Assessment tests taken, from their respective producers |
| Activity grid | The shared 365-day grid from Progress, with the same empty and unavailable states |
| Certificates | The learner's lifetime certificate collection with true current statuses |
| Recent solutions | The newest five accepted-solution entries and a route to Solutions |

Each region has a named source. Do not total raw submissions from other domains here or inspect their private source, answers or files to invent a figure. A displayed saved-solution count may include separate accepted-language entries; label it as such instead of calling it a unique-problem total. The sum of a difficulty breakdown's segments must match its displayed total.

## Identity and level display

Name, email and avatar are projected from the main site. Labs has no name, email, avatar, biography or password editor. Route identity changes to the appropriate main-site destination. A missing name uses Learner where a name is required and a name-free greeting where that reads better.

Use the permitted main-site avatar when it loads. Otherwise use an initial-letter avatar, including an initial from Learner when no display name is available. Failure to load an image is not failure of the whole profile.

The XP row states lifetime total, next-level threshold and remaining XP from Economy. The fill and the wide-layout level ring express the same within-level progress. A new learner sees Level 1 with the next threshold, not zero divided by zero. At Level 100, show Economy's full cap state and Highest level reached while XP continues accumulating. No Bronze/Silver/Gold tier is invented.

An unreadable progress denominator means no misleading fill. An XP failure does not reveal Credit controls while generated help is disabled.

## Certificate collection

Present one lifetime collection without separate written/video prestige rankings or conflicting issuer-source policies. Each item names its awarding learning item and current status. Its actions come from [Certificates](../Certificates/Certificates.md).

| State | Available experience |
|---|---|
| Issued and visible | Download, copy the verification link and Hide with its consequence explained |
| Issued and hidden | Keep owner download and true status; offer Unhide and explain the public link's privacy behavior |
| Awaiting a name | Show the pending reason and Set certificate name; no invented download |
| Generating | Show that production is pending; offer no file until it exists |
| Generation failed | Explain a platform generation problem, not a learner mistake |
| Revoked | Show the status without a download |

The certificate display-name control follows Certificates' existing rules: editable before first generation begins, locked afterwards, with a proposal of the learner's exact corrected name for authorized approval. Pending, approved, rejected and unreadable proposal/name states remain distinct. A rejected proposal leaves the existing name intact.

Hide and Show are reversible privacy choices, not revocation. There is no learner Revoke button. Course edits do not rewrite an issued award; separately authorized certificate correction or invalidation retains its own explicit rules. A failed collection read does not display No certificates.

## Interactions and failures

Learners can open source-owned details, inspect a certificate, open a recent solution, enter Settings and refresh Profile. Refresh rereads the sections, announces its busy state and does not blank usable information.

Regions load independently. A failed region explains what did not load while its neighbors remain usable. After a failed refresh, retain the last confirmed information with its freshness and identify the failed areas. Only a complete first-load failure with nothing usable becomes a full-page error. Unresolvable identity means Profile unavailable, not an apparently empty account.

Every figure retains its producer's freshness and quality label. A genuine zero is shown as zero; an unreadable result is not. Activity detail that expired uses the owning domain's explanation. Navigation away must not let late responses repaint another page.

## Privacy, staff and boundaries

No learner can open another learner's Profile, Skills, preferences, notes or record. There is no public profile, directory, shared profile link, rank or peer comparison. Certificate verification remains the explicit, limited public exception under Certificates' privacy rules.

Administration's separately authorized and audited support view may expose only its approved support facts. It is not a general view of every Profile producer and grants no access to learner Notes, submitted source or project files. Profile adds no staff editing of identity, rewards or skill judgments.

Visiting Profile earns no learning time, XP or achievement. Billing, plan management, session management, broad account export and placement-evidence reports are not introduced here.

## Completion checks

Check a new account, missing name/avatar, the level cap, a failed XP source, distinct saved-entry/problem counts, the five recent solutions and each certificate state. Compare shared activity and skill figures with their owners. Break one refresh and then the complete first load. Verify that another account cannot open the profile or its private linked records.
